InkSlate Pro (In-App Purchases)

InkSlate offers optional InkSlate Pro access through Apple In-App Purchase. Notes, Budget, Calendar, and Settings remain free without purchase.

Pro unlocks: Mind Maps, Journal, To-Do, Recipes, Places, Quotes, and Watchlist.

Plans:

  • InkSlate Pro Monthly — auto-renewing subscription, billed monthly after any free trial.
  • InkSlate Pro Yearly — auto-renewing subscription, billed yearly after any free trial.
  • InkSlate Pro Lifetime — one-time non-consumable purchase with permanent Pro access on devices signed in with the same Apple ID.

Monthly and yearly plans may include a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Pricing is shown in the app before you confirm a purchase.

Auto-renewal: Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Free trial: If a free trial is offered, any unused portion is forfeited when you purchase a subscription, where applicable.

Restore purchases: Use Restore Purchases on the InkSlate Pro screen (Settings → Upgrade to InkSlate Pro) to restore Pro access on a new device or after reinstalling.

Existing lifetime customers: Users who previously purchased InkSlate Pro Lifetime retain permanent access; that purchase is not replaced or removed by the introduction of subscription plans.

Apple handles all payment processing. Refund requests are handled by Apple according to App Store policies.
